ParkSignal AI — Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. Updated November 19, 2026. This policy describes the free v1 release.

ParkSignal reads parking signs on your device and helps you save spots, cars, photos, rules, parking sessions and reminders. ParkSignal does not send your photos, recognized text, location or other personal data to any third-party AI service. We do not operate a server that receives your parking records. The app has no app account, advertising or analytics SDK.

On-device processing and permissions

When you take a sign photo or select a photo for scanning, Apple Vision recognizes its text on the device. ParkSignal interprets that text locally and presents suggested rules for you to review. Photos you add to a saved spot and scans you save become part of your parking records. If you grant access while using the app, ParkSignal uses your location to place spots and show your position. Apple Maps and address lookup may send coordinates or other location-related information to Apple to display maps or look up addresses. You can manage camera, photo and location access in iOS Settings. Reminders and alarms use the device’s notification or alarm permissions. Their preferences and scheduling records stay on that device.

iCloud and backups

When iCloud is available and enabled for ParkSignal, SwiftData/CloudKit is configured to synchronize your saved parking records and photo data through your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. This means those records and photos can leave the device for Apple's iCloud service. The developer does not receive a copy on a separate server. Sync can be delayed or unavailable; Settings shows storage status. Do not treat this as a guarantee that every item has been backed up until you have verified it on another device.

Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple Maps, address lookup, iCloud and Apple’s device-backup services. Your iCloud and device-backup settings control those features. Turning off iCloud does not automatically delete existing cloud copies. Deleting the app removes its local storage but may leave iCloud data or system backups that can restore later.

Retention and deletion

Your saved records remain until you delete them. Delete spots, scans or photos in the app; when sync is working, the associated record deletions can propagate to your other devices. If you want to remove iCloud copies, also use Apple's iCloud storage controls. Backup retention is controlled by Apple and your account settings. We cannot retrieve or delete your private CloudKit records from a developer server.

Support and sharing

Support is available at https://github.com/shawn8989/ParkSignalAIBeta/issues. Submitting an issue is optional and publishes what you write on GitHub. Do not post license plates, home addresses, precise parking locations, account details or private photos. GitHub's privacy policy applies to information you submit there.

Tracking and future changes

ParkSignal does not include ads, cross-app tracking or sale of personal data. This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change.
