Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a parking sign?

Tap the scan button, point your camera at the sign, and capture it. ParkSignal reads the text on your device and turns it into a color-coded parking signal. You can also pick an existing photo from your library.

What do the colors mean?
  • Green — safe to park now
  • Red — illegal to park right now
  • Yellow — allowed now, but a restriction is starting soon
  • Blue — permit / ADA required
  • Purple — metered / paid parking
  • Gray — unknown or incomplete scan
The app read the sign incorrectly. What can I do?

On-device reading isn't perfect, especially with worn or unusual signs. You can edit any spot's restrictions by hand — open the spot, tap Edit, and adjust the days, times, and type. Always confirm against the posted sign.

How do the alarms work?

When a spot has a restriction, you can set a reminder that fires before the restriction begins, using the lead time you choose. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, so they work without a network connection. Make sure notifications are allowed for ParkSignal in iOS Settings.

Does my data sync between devices?

If you're signed in to iCloud, your saved spots sync across your Apple devices through your private iCloud database. Your data stays in your own iCloud account — it isn't sent to the developer or anyone else. Sync happens when the app is open.

Is my data private?

Yes. ParkSignal has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you scan stays on your device (and your own private iCloud if enabled). See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does ParkSignal guarantee I won't get a ticket?

No. ParkSignal helps you read and organize parking information, but it can't guarantee accuracy or prevent citations. Always confirm the posted signs before you park.
