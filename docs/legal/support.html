ParkSignal Support

A parking-sign reader, organizer and reminder for iPhone.

Report a problem

Open a support issue or read existing reports. Include the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post your license plate, home address, precise parking location, account details or private photos. A GitHub account is needed to post there; none is needed to use ParkSignal.

Scan needs review

Read the original sign and all nearby signs. Complex or unsupported rules stay unresolved. Correct supported weekly rules manually. ParkSignal does not guarantee parking legality or prevent tickets.

A reminder did not fire

Open Settings → Alerts & Alarms in ParkSignal and check permissions and active reminders. Enable parking reminders on each device you use. Review iOS notification settings, Focus settings and the scheduled time. Ending parking, deleting rules or cancelling an alarm removes its reminder.

iCloud and photos

Check Storage & Sync in ParkSignal Settings. An available iCloud account is not proof that every item has uploaded. Verify saved spots and photos on your other device before relying on a backup. If storage cannot be opened, do not delete the app: that could remove local data that has not synced.

Cost and privacy

The free v1 app has no ads, subscription or OpenAI requirement. OCR and sign parsing run locally. Optional iCloud and Apple Maps use Apple services. Read our privacy policy for details.
