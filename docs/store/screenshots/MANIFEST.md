## Provenance and limitations

- Map coordinates are simulator sample locations in San Francisco. Saved records
  are manually created test data, not authoritative rules for those streets.
- `../test-assets/sample-sign.png` is an intentionally labeled synthetic sign,
  generated by its adjacent Swift source. It was imported into the simulator photo
  library and passed through the real photo-picker/Vision OCR flow. It is not a
  photograph of a real curb. No production demo-data seeding or screenshot-only
  app behavior was added.
- Earlier `iphone69-*.png` files outside `release/` are historical. One includes
  DEBUG controls; others show outdated behavior. Do not upload them.
- The target is iPhone-only. Verify Apple's current accepted screenshot dimensions
  and device-family slots in App Store Connect before uploading.
